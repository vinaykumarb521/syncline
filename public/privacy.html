Privacy

Syncline keeps its data on this device. There is no account, no ad network, and no server collecting how you play.

What stays on the device

  • Your best score, today's best, the orb you picked, whether sound is muted, and the last mode you chose. These live in local storage in the browser or app.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer when you tap Share. The share sheet or clipboard is handled by your device.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, location, contacts, or advertising identifier.
  • No gameplay analytics and no third-party trackers.

Clear the site data, or uninstall the app, and the saved scores go with it.
